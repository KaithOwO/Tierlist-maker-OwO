Settings

Library

Supports any image or video your browser can open (GIF, PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, SVG, MP4, WebM, MOV…). Videos are converted to GIFs (max 30 seconds). A sources.txt in the folder (filename | link per line) fills in links automatically.

Export pack

Saves every item (with links and tiers) into one file. Send a .tierpack to someone to use with Import pack. A pack.js loads automatically when it's put next to index.html (on a website), but it's about a third bigger.
